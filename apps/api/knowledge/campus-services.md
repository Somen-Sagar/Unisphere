# Campus Services & Facilities

## Campus Map & Navigation
UniSphere campus facilities are organized into North, South, and Central Quads. The central student administrative building houses Academic Affairs, Student Welfare, and Admissions.

## Library & Learning Resource Center
- Hours: Monday through Saturday, 8:00 AM – 10:00 PM. Sunday: 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM.
- Digital catalogue and quiet study spaces are located on Floors 2 and 3.

## Campus Mess & Dining
- Dining Hall timings:
  - Breakfast: 7:30 AM – 9:30 AM
  - Lunch: 12:00 PM – 2:30 PM
  - Snacks: 4:30 PM – 6:00 PM
  - Dinner: 7:30 PM – 9:30 PM

## Student ID & QR Pass Verification
All registered students are issued an official UniSphere digital pass with a unique QR code. Digital QR passes can be accessed directly from the "My Passes" section of the UniSphere student portal.
