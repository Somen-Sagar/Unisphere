# Academic Policies & Student Guidelines

## Attendance Policy
Students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in each enrolled subject to be eligible for end-semester examinations. Medical exemptions must be submitted to the department coordinator within 7 calendar days.

## Event Participation & Leave of Absence
Participation in official college-approved hackathons, technical symposiums, and cultural events organized through recognized campus clubs qualifies for official duty attendance credit.

## Code of Conduct
All students are expected to adhere to academic integrity guidelines. Plagiarism, unauthorized collaboration on graded assessments, or disruptive conduct on campus is strictly prohibited.
