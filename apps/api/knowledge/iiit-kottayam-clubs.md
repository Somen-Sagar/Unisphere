# IIIT Kottayam Clubs & Campus Life Knowledge Base

This repository contains official information regarding student clubs, faculty in-charge (FICs), mentors, sub-clubs, facilities, and key events at the Indian Institute of Information Technology (IIIT) Kottayam.

---

  - Chitrachaya (Photography club)
  - Literary Club (Quizzes, debates, and literature)

## 4. Cyber Security Club

Focused on protecting digital assets, exploring open-source cybersecurity tools, network defense, cryptography, and ethical hacking.

- **Department Head (Cyber Security)**: Dr. Panchami V (Course Coordinator & Point of Contact for the Cyber Commando Training program)
- **Notable Events & Initiatives**:
  - **Apoorv Capture The Flag (CTF)**: An annual strategy, cryptography, and teamwork competition hosted during the techno-cultural fest
  - **Hacktoberfest Contributions**: Hands-on live open-source sprints focusing on Git, GitHub, and cipher projects
  - **Eco-Crypt**: A cipher project celebrating Environment Day by encrypting ASCII characters into emojis
  - **Technical Webinars**: Special sessions on Web3 security, smart contracts auditing, and drone threat detection

## 6. Flagship & Recurring Campus Events

While weekly schedules are posted on campus notice boards, the major recurring annual events are:

- **APOORV**: The premier annual techno-cultural fest featuring live concerts, Capture The Flag (CTF) competitions, tech exhibitions, and creative workshops.
- **Adavya**: The official freshers' welcome night hosted by the Social Club (Trendles Club).
- **Hackathons & Coding Sprints**: Continuous preparation for Smart India Hackathon (SIH) and autumn Hacktoberfest open-source sprints.
- **Inter-IIIT Sports Meets (Twaran)**: Annual inter-college sports tournaments held across various IIIT campuses during the winter/spring semesters.
